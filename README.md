# My Favorite Movie: Avengers: Endgame

**Avengers: Endgame** (2019) is an *epic superhero film* produced by Marvel Studios and serves as the grand conclusion to the Infinity Saga.

## Why I Love This Movie
* **Iconic Climax:** The final battle scene where all the heroes assemble is unforgettable.
* **Emotional Depth:** Tony Stark's sacrifice and Steve Rogers' ending gave a satisfying closure to their character arcs.
* **Great Action & Visuals:** Top-tier cinematography and CGI from start to finish.

## Favorite Characters
1. Iron Man (Tony Stark)
2. Captain America (Steve Rogers)
3. Thor

> "I am Iron Man." - Tony Stark
